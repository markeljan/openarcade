They are an inventory of published metadata, not evidence that every game was inspected or recreated. See [REA-INVESTIGATION.md](REA-INVESTIGATION.md) for the bounded Machine Hunt inspection and its limitations.

All eighty reference games have local, independently implemented approximations listed below. These are simplified interpretations, not verified reproductions of reference gameplay. Other local games are independent entries, and genre or name similarity alone does not establish a match. The linked investigation and fidelity priorities describe bounded inspections of selected games; this catalog does not establish complete behavior for every game. Catalog status reflects the local source as inspected on 2026-10-28.

## Feature comparison

| Feature | Reference observed | Local implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Catalog | 80 named games | 112 games; eighty are simplified interpretations of reference entries |
| Gallery | Wall and horizontal row | Wall and horizontal row, with navigation and keyboard selection |
| Previews | Up to six visible games run at 30 FPS; stills for reduced motion | Up to six visible instances run, with previews throttled to about 30 FPS; still frames for reduced motion |
| Playing | Opens a portrait game, instructions then scored run | Opens a portrait player, instructions, manual play, restart and switch to AI |
| Progress and polish | Game-specific art, sound, scoring, game-over states and stored best scores | Simplified art and game-specific scoring; six classic bonus games, Machine Hunt, Meat Lad, Turbo Arena, Jelly Dash, Side Surfers, Word Fall, Rune Swarm, Web Slam, Deadline Deck, Scrap Arena, Gravity Claw, Star Roller and opt-in custom games persist best scores; most reference-inspired games do not yet share that behavior |
| Adding games | Not assessed | Scaffold and automatically discovered custom modules |
| Recording | Available | Omitted as requested |

The local previews are lightweight rule-based agents, not language models. They do not require network calls or a model API. This comparison captures the observed concept and known differences; it is not a gameplay equivalence test for the full reference catalog.


## Known gaps in selected approximations

- Deep Digger has timed tool-dependent mining, durability and repair, depth-banded ores, a beacon and a 100 m golem. Fluids, caves, combat and later goals remain simplified; Rune Swarm has stripe, prism and nova match pieces but simplifies combat spells.
- Web Slam now includes physical platforms and a momentum-based swing but simplifies knockback. Deadline Deck includes grind balance, combo banking and a route grade, but simplifies collisions and route events.
- Scrap Arena omits the full upgrade systems and deeper vehicle physics. Gridiron Rush includes basic first downs and ramps but omits detailed formations and championship progression.
- Ghost Shift now uses ordered sigils and patrolling sentries but omits detailed layouts; Wingrush simplifies hill physics and fort components.
- Grave Garden has timed wave rosters, four plant roles, lane defenses, breaches and a blood moon; gardener movement, planning and presentation remain simplified; Swarm Keeper has basic bridge, cargo and rescue work, while roles and beetle combat remain simplified or absent.
- Cosmic Haul now has tether stress and visual Ark modules but simplifies chain dynamics; Midnight Pursuit has garage swaps and roadblocks but omits helicopters, alleys and detailed car classes.
- Pico Cap cycles through five parameterized rooms; Frog Convoy checks follower hazards and guard but simplifies moving-log physics and seasonal events.
- Tower Panic has a ladder graph, rolling barrels, cyclic fire and machinery, cascade warnings and timed extraction; AI risk assessment and presentation remain simplified. Burrow Boss has fixed shoring sites, delayed cave-ins, boss health and arena progression; pathfinding and hazards remain simplified.
- Idol Escape uses timed rock, cart, water, seal and artifact interactions with nonfatal chamber transitions, but simplifies layouts and chain physics; Robot Register runs five registers across three courses with timed reveals, simultaneous movement and machinery; program personalities and presentation remain simplified.

- Castle Crasher adds crews, wind and debris but simplifies angular physics; Hotel Haunt varies furnishings and ghost strength but repeats the underlying layout.
- Kaiju Control now requires evacuation trips but simplifies road routing; Moonshine Valley has crop and ward progression but simplifies seasons and combat.
- Dungeon Relay generates connected mazes and displays a prior-run trail, but simplifies the reference room graph and ghost timing; Gravity Claw has cell-locked ascent, charged throws, catchable bolts and a tripod boss, but simplifies zone generation, physics and later hazards.

- Ricochet Foundry has ordered machine operations but simplifies charge banking; Lantern Line routes motes through individual modules but simplifies production and refraction.
- Ashen Rage has rival attacks and pickups but simplifies racing and weapons; Star Roller has a pursuing sweeper but simplifies world physics and scripted events.
- Dragon Lock has biome travel, timed acts and phased bosses but simplifies altitude and weak-point physics; Cursed Fist has air attacks and juggling but simplifies combat and maps.
- Coral Courier has oxygen bubbles and multiple reef palettes but simplifies habitats; Beacon Bay has shoals and tides but simplifies convoy and harbor physics.

- Railway Rush has spatial tracks, coupling and train contact but simplifies routing and collisions; Pottery House has clay, wheel, glaze, ash, fuel, vent, kiln, water and shelf interactions with cracks and firing quality; timing, art and hazard detail are simplified.
- Hive Heist now sends a worker to collect nectar but simplifies brood behavior; Cloud Caravan models cargo shift and nonfatal hull damage but not individual crate collisions.
- Orchard Wardens includes grafts and frost shelters but simplifies maturation; Avalanche Post has hiker rescue and lodge delivery but simplifies dogs, ropes and switchback terrain.

- Orbit Kitchen now has basic recipes, tethered movement and food-stealing drones but omits several stations; Hearth Conductor grades cue timing and uses synthesized tones but omits featured solos and sampled instruments.

- Rune Rebound adds a guided ritual orb and crests but simplifies pinball physics; Pocket Regatta has contact penalties and yielding but simplifies right-of-way and sailing physics.
- Willow Dam now has drifting logs, kit rescues and water surges; Storm Roundup adds rope tension and telegraphed lightning, but both simplify their environmental physics.

- Drift Divers models oxygen, hull hazards and tether strain but simplifies submarine puzzles; Lantern Lift has stranded rescue and sheave thaw but simplifies routes and balance.
- Dune Delivery carries water to dry wells but simplifies navigation; Fossil Frontier has nine finds, matching sockets and fragment repair but simplifies stability and rockfall.
- Starlight Ward traces reflected beams but simplifies optics and level progression; Rooftop Post now has gaps, ladders and cats but simplifies their behavior and animation.

- Bakery Blitz has proofing, baking and spoilage but simplifies loaf quality; Starfall Station includes an oxygen-bay EVA but simplifies modules and shield interception.
- Greenhouse Guardians has six species, routed valves, shutters and grafts but simplifies species behavior and plumbing; Harbor Hoist tracks manifests and tides and has crossing workers, while cargo physics and rehandling remain simplified.
- Tower Heist uses moving gear alignment but simplifies transitions; Storm Slot has three circuit themes and cup trophies but simplifies track geometry and championship detail.

- Glacier Glide now separates fish feeding and chick rescue and has a moving floe at an ice gap; Cloud Shepherd has distinct follower speeds, storms and a functional bridge, while both simplify the reference physics.
- Essence Workshop now uses recipes and coil/curse maintenance but simplifies ingredients; Mill Mechanic adds belt stress, crow sabotage and separate systems but does not simulate rigid gears.
- Wobble War now has knockback, projectiles and ice/lava hazards but simplifies combat physics; Void Gambit has named encounters, warned salvos, moving ships, projectile contact and upgrades, but simplifies hull shapes and timing.
- Rulebound includes six authored pages, alternate starts, moth turns and 22-frame move commits; its default orientation follows the inspected reference while optional mirroring remains available. Art and alternative-route coverage remain incomplete; Hex Hamlet has connected-region quests but simplifies terrain and vehicle deliveries.

- Ashen Courier has authored guard encounters, physical boon shrines and a guardian finale but simplifies geometry and combat details; Icebound Ascent has variable rooms and checkpoints but simplifies authored layouts and hazards.
- Phase Foundry transfers momentum through oriented exits across three chambers but simplifies geometry and reverse transfer; Hearthbound has working colonists and seasonal hazards but simplifies logistics and evacuation.
- Gloam Trawler now has relics and night threats but simplifies light and shadow; Reef to Rice tracks fish species and predators but simplifies recipes and capture physics.
- Neon Duelist has three side-view rooms, one-hit rewind and a returned-shot captain finale, but simplifies geometry, targeting and presentation; Street Crew has routed traffic and bridges but uses discrete grid movement.
- Stamp Harbor has progressive shift rules, separate documents and station-based inspection, stamping and ledger interactions; its documents and presentation remain simplified. Wash Wonder has three restoration jobs, material-specific nozzles, pressure, refills and gates; its grime masks, art and animation are simplified.


- Machine Hunt has multiple enemy types, a Thunderjaw, loot, biome palettes and a scored game-over panel; its world generation and machine behavior remain simplified. Meat Lad and the reference both use procedural sections; local generation, solvability checks, hazards and rescue physics differ.
- Turbo Arena simplifies drift and vehicle collisions; Jelly Dash has a simplified multi-round elimination tournament with varied courses, but its courses and full tournament behavior differ from the reference.
- Side Surfers lacks authored layouts and full powerup physics; Word Fall has charged platform altars, starter guesses and letter powers, but omits several weapons, enemies and hazards.


## Selected behavioral checks

These are scoped simulations with mocked canvases, not equivalence tests against the live games. They supplement the browser player-flow checks and the automated lifecycle suite.

- Machine Hunt's manual run reached a scored game-over screen; Meat Lad's manual movement and wall jump were exercised.
- A scripted Block Mine counterpart run crafted a pick and defeated a golem. Robo Rally's counterpart queued and executed a three-card program.
- Beacon Bay autoplay saved boats and a manual scripted run saved two; Cloud Shepherd's scripted flock could cross after its bridge was activated.
- Manual checks verified a changed Hex Hamlet score after rotating a tile, and changed Starlight Ward beam paths after turning mirrors and its prism.
- Pottery House autoplay completed pots through shaping and kiln temperature control; Rooftop Post manual checks exercised ladders and an unjumped gap fall.
- Automated checks cover rendering, keyboard/pointer dispatch and lifecycle cleanup over simulated preview frames, but do not establish that every scoring path or long run is correct.


- On the updated local build, all 80 reference-inspired entries completed a browser smoke flow: open, start, directional and action input, restart, switch to AI, and close. This checks the shared player path, not each game's full win conditions or parity.

- A subsequent isolated Edge keyboard replay completed Pottery House's clay, wheel, glaze, ash, firing, water cooling and shelf display; it showed `IRIDESCENT ASH REVEAL` and `GALLERY 1`. The detailed corresponding reference flow is still unverified.
- A later 60-second seeded autoplay audit completed without a reported script error. This finite simulation is not evidence of long-run progress or reference equivalence. Current per-game static evidence and open gaps are recorded in [REA-INVESTIGATION.md](REA-INVESTIGATION.md).
